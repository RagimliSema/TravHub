Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal data TravHub (travhub-tours.onrender.com) collects when you create an account or book a tour, how it is used and how you can have it deleted.

Data we collect

  • Account: your name and email address. If you register with a password, only a secure hash of it is stored – never the password itself.
  • Google or Facebook sign-in: if you choose it, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from that service. We do not receive your Google or Facebook password and we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Bookings: your wishlist, cart and orders, and the contact details you enter at checkout (full name, phone, address, city, country, postal code).
  • Technical data: the server briefly processes your IP address to protect the login and password-reset forms from abuse.

How we use it

  • to create your account and let you sign in;
  • to show your wishlist, cart and orders and to process your bookings;
  • to send you a password reset link when you request one.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

Cookies and browser storage

After you sign in, a login token is kept in your browser (local storage if you choose “Remember me”, otherwise until the tab is closed). During Google or Facebook sign-in a short security cookie is used for up to 10 minutes. Signing out removes the token.

Services that process data for us

  • Render – hosts the website and the server.
  • MongoDB Atlas – stores the database.
  • Brevo – sends password reset emails.
  • Google and Facebook – only if you choose to sign in with them.

How long we keep data

Your account data is kept until your account is deleted. Password reset links expire after 15 minutes and can be used only once. Past orders may remain in the order history without your account.

Deleting your data

To delete your TravHub account and the data linked to it, email ragimlisema18@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my TravHub account”. Your account, wishlist and cart are deleted within 30 days and you receive a confirmation.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove TravHub in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, then send the email above.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or to delete it at any time by writing to ragimlisema18@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
